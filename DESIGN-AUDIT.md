## Review method

Each homepage is exercised in Chrome. The checks cover the topic selector, 3D canvas creation, manual rotation, expandable reading notes and text download. Viewport widths are checked at 390 and 1280 pixels. Local links and assets are checked across the gallery and all six pages of every concept. This covers the tested browser and dimensions. Booking, purchasing and client intake are outside these independent portfolio demonstrations.
